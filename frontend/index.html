Question 1: System-1 (Decision Models) vs. System-2 (Frontier LLM) Trade-offs

Decision models (/api/alpha/decisions) operate at millisecond latencies (~35–115ms), excelling at high-throughput reactive execution. Frontier LLMs (Claude Opus 5.5) represent deliberative System-2 planning: higher per-move latency (~1100ms) but superior move efficiency and strategic goal-directedness on complex states.

Question 2: Why Optimality Gap Must Be Paired with Success Rate (Survivorship Bias)

Evaluating models solely on Optimality Gap conditions exclusively on solved trials. A fragile model that solves 1 of 5 puzzles in 4 moves has a deceptive Gap of 0.0 despite an 80% failure rate, whereas a robust model solving 5 of 5 in 6 moves has Gap +2.0. Optimality Gap measures precision among successes; Success Rate measures robustness across the distribution.

Question 3: Error Rates & Schema Drift (/alpha/decisions vs. /v1/chat/completions)

Decision models demonstrate near-zero malformed responses because /api/alpha/decisions frames move selection as a discrete logit-masked classification over predefined candidate criteria. In contrast, /chat/completions is an unconstrained autoregressive token generator that can suffer from conversational leakage, schema drift, or hallucinated move notation (e.g., 'R3', 'Right') under spatial ambiguity.

Question 4: Scalability Limits of Exact BFS vs. Heuristic Solvers

Exact BFS shortest-path solving scales as O(18 * 15^(d-1)). For primary benchmark scrambles (3–5 moves), bidirectional BFS explores under 10,000 states in <25ms. However, at 15–20 moves, the state space explodes to >10^17 states, exceeding available physical RAM. For extension tiers, exact BFS must be supplemented with Kociemba's Two-Phase or IDA* heuristic pattern databases.

Question 5: Real-World Robotics & Spatial Deployment Recommendations

For practical real-time robotic systems, a hierarchical hybrid architecture is optimal: deploy fast Decision models for high-frequency reactive execution (System-1), falling back to a deliberative Frontier LLM or heuristic solver (System-2) only when state cycle detection triggers or progress stalls.
